SPIDEY COMPACT - R8 MOTOR-DRIVER WAKE FIX
REV: R8-0R (replaces prior C1 100k R8 BOM)

MANDATORY ECO / DO NOT MISS:
  - FIT R8 with one (1) 0-ohm 0603 SMD jumper, Yageo RC0603JR-070RL.
  - This replaces the original 100k resistor (RC0603FR-07100KL) at R8.
  - Do not fit 100k, do not leave R8 unpopulated, and do not add a separate
    hand-solder bridge if a 0-ohm jumper is fitted.
  - R8 connects SYS to U2 DRV8838 nSLEEP. The original 100k was too weak
    against the driver's internal pulldown and held nSLEEP too low.
  - Prototype confirmed: at SYS 3.3V, original nSLEEP 1.2V; after bridging
    R8, motor works. Verify nSLEEP approximately SYS in powered QA.
  - If exact 0-ohm 0603 part is unavailable, request customer approval for
    an equivalent 0-ohm 0603 part; never substitute a 100k resistor.

OTHER ASSEMBLY REQUIREMENTS:
  - Turnkey PCBA, TOP SIDE components only, 22mm x 20mm PCB.
  - PCBWay to panelize as needed for assembly, with rails and panel fiducials.
  - Do not substitute ANY parts without customer approval.
  - Verify orientation/polarity of D1L, D1H, D2 and C6.
  - USB connector J1: HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12 / LCSC C165948, SMT contacts and
    through-hole shell retention tabs.
  - U2: DRV8838DSGR WSON-8, exposed GND pad; verify stencil/paste treatment.
  - C8 is DNP (optional motor suppression capacitor).
  - J2-J7 are PCB solder/test pads only. External BAT, SW, PWR, MOTOR, SPKR,
    UPDI pads are intentionally UNPOPULATED and require no purchased parts.
  - BOM specifies 22 FIT components including R8. CPL lists exactly these 22.

PCB BARE-BOARD SETTINGS:
  22 x 20mm, 2 layers, FR-4, 1.6mm, 1oz, green solder mask,
  white silkscreen, lead-free HASL. PCBWay panelization allowed.
